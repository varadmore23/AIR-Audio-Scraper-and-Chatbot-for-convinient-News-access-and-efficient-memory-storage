The Gold Rush continued for India at the Asian Games in Naitrena, Goen, Japan as As Indian athletes delivered a stellar performance on day 13, clinching six gold medals in hockey, boxing, wrestling and archery in a spectacular display of skill, determination and sporting excellence. Indian women's hockey team clinched the gold medal, defeating China 1-0 in the closely fought final. Lalrensema Ihmar Zote scored the decisive loan goals for India, while goalkeeper Savita Punea produced a crucial save from a penalty stroke to preserve the lead and secure the gold medal. The victory marks India's first women hockey gold at the Asian Games in 44 years, ending a long wait for the title. Earlier Indian wrestler Sujit Kalkal scripted a remarkable comeback to win the gold medal in the men's 65 kilogram freestyle event. In boxing, Parveen Huda won the gold medal in the women's 65 kg category, defeating Chinese Taipei's Nian Chin Chen 3-2 in a split decision in the final. Ankush West Bengal clinched the gold medal in the men's 80kg category, defeating South Korea's Kim Min Se-yong by a split decision 4-0 in the final. India star woman boxer Lovlina Burguhain also clinched a gold medal in the 75kg category, defeating China's Bao Zi by a unanimous 5-0 decision in the final. In archery, women's recurve team created history by clinching its made-in-Asian games gold medal, the Tri of Ankita Bhagat, Kirti Mishra and Kumkum Hoot defeated world champion South Korea 5-3 in the thrilling final. India also backed silver in the mixed recurve team event, Kumkum Hoot and Dheeraj Bommadevra finished runners-up after going down 3-5 to China in a closely contested final. Sipak Thakraw, India scored the bronze medal in the men's quadrant event. In sailing, Netra Kumanan clinched bronze in the women's dengue event. With these impressive performances, India has climbed to fifth position in the overall medal standings. The country's medal tally has risen to 76, including 16 gold, 25 silver and 35 bronze medals. President Draupadi Murmur and Prime Minister Narendra Modi have congratulated the medal winners.
