World Stroke Day is being observed today to raise awareness about stroke and the importance of recognizing its warning signs. On the occasion, Union Minister for Health and Family Welfare Jagat Prakashna Das said, sudden facial drooping, weakness in an arm, or difficulty speaking, should never be ignored. Talking to Akashwani News, Chief of the Neurosciences Centre at All India Institute of Medical Sciences Ames, New Delhi, Dr. Shailesh B. Gaikwad said, The symptoms of the stroke include sudden loss or blurring of vision, double vision, loss of balance, and difficulty in walking. Dr. Gayakwar emphasized, if such symptoms are visible, then the patient should immediately transfer to stroke-ready hospital for treatment. There are some symptoms which should be recognized by the general people. The term FAST has been propagated by the World Stroke Organization to create awareness in order to recognize the earliest signs of the stroke. In fast, the F stands for face where the face is stroking on one side, A stands for arm or leg where there is a sudden onset of weakness, S stands for difficulty in speech, T stands for time to act and reach to a hospital which is stroked ready.
