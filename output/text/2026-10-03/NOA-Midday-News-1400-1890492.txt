In the field of health security, Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojna is a fully government-funded health assurance scheme. Under this, eligible families receive a health cover of up to 5 lakh rupees every year. The scheme offers cashless treatment facilities for secondary and tertiary care in empaneled public and private hospitals across the country. Pre-hospitalization expenses for up to three days and post-discharge expenses for up to 15 days are also included. Pre-existing conditions are covered from day one of the scheme. There is no limit on family size, age or gender under the scheme. Eligible beneficiaries can avail of the benefits at any empaneled hospital in any state state across the country, meaning beneficiaries are not restricted to their home state for treatment. Furthermore, all senior citizens aged 70 years and above, regardless of their income or socioeconomic status are eligible for a health cover of up to 5 lakh rupees under Ayushman Bharat PMJ.
