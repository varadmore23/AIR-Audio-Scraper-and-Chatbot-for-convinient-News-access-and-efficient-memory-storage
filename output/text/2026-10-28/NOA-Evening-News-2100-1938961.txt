The government today approved several key initiatives aimed at strengthening domestic manufacturing and promoting rural governance. The decisions were taken at a union cabinet meeting chaired by Prime Minister Narendra Modi. Briefing near New Delhi, Information and Broadcasting Minister Ashwini Vaishnava announced the details. Mr Vaishnav said the cabinet has approved the container manufacturing promotion scheme with a total outlay of 10,000 crore rupees over 13 years up to the financial year 2039-40. The scheme aims to establish a globally competitive container manufacturing ecosystem in India. Containers are a major fulcrum of trade for India and the world. The container manufacturing scheme that the prime minister has approved in the cabinet includes elements that, in the coming few years, India will emerge as a new destination for container manufacturing. The Cabinet has also given the nod to the Kelo-India Sports Goods Manufacturing Scheme to position India as a global hub for high-quality sports goods. This is a long-term scheme for sports goods spanning an eight-year period. It has an outlay of Rs 1,856.67 crore and will remain operational from fiscal year 2026-27 to fiscal year 2033-34. It has two pillars. The first pillar consists of incentives to support manufacturing. The second pillar consists of incentives to develop the entire ecosystem. The Cabinet also cleared the Pratamantri Vikasit Gram Thvaraj Yojana as a centrally sponsored scheme with a total financial outlay of 7,395 crore rupees. The scheme will be implemented over five years from 2026-2027 to 2031 to empower gram panchayats and strengthen grass roots governance.
