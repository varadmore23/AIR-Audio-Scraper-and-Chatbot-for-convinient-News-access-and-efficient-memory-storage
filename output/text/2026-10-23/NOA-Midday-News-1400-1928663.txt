The Allahabad High Court has ruled that a survivor of sexual assault under the Poxo Act cannot be denied compensation merely because the medical report does not record physical injuries. A division bench of Justice Shekhar B. Sarraf and Justice Amitabh Kumar Rai directed authorities to pay 3 lakh rupees to a minor under the Uttar Pradesh Rani Lakshmi Bhai Mahila Samman Kosh scheme within 10 days of submission of a certified copy of the order. The court set aside the district steering committee's decision to withhold compensation observing that physical injuries are not a necessary condition for establishing penetrative sexual assault under Section 3 of the Boxer Act. It held that the absence of injury marks cannot by itself negate an allegation of sexual assault. The bench noted that once the FIR and charge sheet indicate an offense under Section 4 of the Act, compensation cannot be denied solely on the basis of a medical report that requires no injuries.
