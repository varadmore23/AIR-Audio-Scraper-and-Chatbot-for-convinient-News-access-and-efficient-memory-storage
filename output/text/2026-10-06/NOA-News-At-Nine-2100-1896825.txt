The Union Cabinet has approved the setting up of Integrated Transport and Logistics Authority to streamline multimodal planning. Briefing media in New Delhi today after the Cabinet meeting, chaired by Prime Minister Narendra Modi, information and broadcasting Minister Ashwini Vaishnav said the body will prepare a long-term national transport master plan with a horizon of 10 years. He said it will serve as the apex body for integrated transport and logistic planning in the country. As the country's GDP is growing at a rate of around 7-8%, the demand for logistics is also continuously increasing. A large number of highways are being constructed across the country along with new railway lines, waterways, ports and airports. To ensure that all these developments are planned in an integrated and unified manner, the Prime Minister launched the PM Gati Shakti National Master Plan three years ago. Now, keeping future requirements in mind, an integrated transport and logistics authority will be established to study and plan all these aspects. Mr. Vaishnav said the Cabinet has also given note to the establishment of Rs. 10,000 crore small and medium enterprises growth fund for equity investments in small and medium enterprises. A 10,000 crore rupees MSME growth fund will be established to provide long-term equity capital to MSMEs with strong growth potential. The fund will be operated with the support of the Ministry of MSME and the Ministry of Finance and will be professionally managed to ensure effective allocation of capital. The fund will place a strong emphasis on manufacturing-focused MSMEs with nearly half of its corpus expected to be directed towards the manufacturing sector. This focus aims to support MSMEs in leveraging the significant growth opportunities emerging in the sector. Prime Minister Narendra Modi in a social media post said, this cabinet decision is a big boost for MSME sector and a major step towards creating the next generation of Indian business leaders. He added that the integrated transport and logistics authority approved by the cabinet will bring greater coordination, data-led planning and multimodal integration across India's transport ecosystem. The Ministry of Defence has signed a contract worth over Rs 660 crore with BrahMos Aerospace Private Limited for procurement of BrahMos fire control system and launchers for Indian Navy ships.
