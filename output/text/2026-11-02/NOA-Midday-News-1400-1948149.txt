The government has notified the Vikesit Bharat Guarantee for Rosgarh and RGB Commission Gramine, VBG Ramji, grievance redressal rules, 2026 establishing a structured multi-tier and time-bound mechanism for addressing grievances relating to the implementation of the scheme. The Ministry of Rural Development said the rules aim to make grievance redressal more accessible, transparent and accountable, while ensuring that citizens' concerns receive timely attention action and appropriate action, a report. The ministry said that the rules provide for the establishment of a time-bound multi-tier grievance redressal mechanism by state governments at the Gram Panchayat block and district levels. It said that designated grievance redressal officers at the appropriate levels will be responsible for receiving, registering, inquiring into and disposing of grievances. To facilitate easy access, any person may submit a grievance relating to any matter are rising out of or connected with the implementation of the scheme. The rules provide for submission of grievances in person before the designated Grievance Redressal Officer through speed post with proof of delivery through the digital public infrastructure for the Dressal of Grievances designated by the central government and through telephone. The rules also prescribe clear timelines to ensure prompt examination and resolution of grievances. The Ministry of Defence today signed contracts worth over 53,800 crore rupees with garden reach, shipbuilders and engineers limited and Goa shipyard limited for the acquisition of 8 next generation corvettes for the Indian Navy.
