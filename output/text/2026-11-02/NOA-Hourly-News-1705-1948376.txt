Ahead of the festival of Diwali on 8th of this month, the Supreme Court has allowed the bursting of green-fire crackers in Delhi NCR from 6am to 7am and from 8pm to 10pm on 7th and 8th November. The court, however, has directed that there shall not be any bursting of fireworks within a 300-metre radius of hospitals, nursing homes, primary and district health centres, old age homes and any notified silence zones. It also directed that there shall be no manufacture or sale of firecrackers joined in searies and garlands and reiterated that the use of fire crackers with barium and those not approved by the CSIR neary as green crackers shall not be permitted. The top court further said that the complete ban on firecrackers in Delhi NCR as imposed earlier shall be restored with effect from 9th of November. He said local villagers had arranged a concrete mixer to construct a road on their own. Prime Minister Narendra Modi has expressed grief over the loss of lives in Jhanshanu District.
