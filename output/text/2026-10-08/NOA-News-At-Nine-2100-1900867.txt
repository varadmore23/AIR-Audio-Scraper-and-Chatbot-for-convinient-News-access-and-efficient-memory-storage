The GST Council during its 57th meeting in New Delhi today approved a series of measures to ease compliance for taxpayers. The decisions include scrapping the arrest powers of tax officers under the GST regime regime and raising the threshold for prosecution from Rs.5 crore to Rs.1 crore. Finance Minister Irmala Sita Raman made the announcement while addressing the media in the national capital this evening. The council recommended removing arrest powers under GST. Council recommended raising the prosecution threshold from Rs.1 crore to Rs.5 crore and reducing the general penalty from Rs.25,000 to Rs.10,000. The punishment whether a fine imprisonment or both will be left to judicial discretion in each case. A taxpayer who files a return late, makes a mistake or falls behind on payment will face recovery proceedings, interest and a proportionate penalty.
