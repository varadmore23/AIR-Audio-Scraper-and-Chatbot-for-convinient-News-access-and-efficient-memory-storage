The government has constituted a committee to examine issues raised by sugarcane farmers and recommend appropriate measures for addressing their concerns. Former member of Nitiayok, Professor Ramesh Chand, will chair the committee which included Agriculture Commissioner, Joint Secretary Suga, Department of Food and Public Distribution and Joint Secretary Legal Metrology, Department of Consumer Affairs as members. The constitution of the committee follows the decisions taken during a meeting of representatives of sugarcane farmers from Karnataka and Maharashtra with the Union Minister of Agriculture and Farmers' Welfare and the Union Minister of Consumer affairs, food and public distribution. More from a correspondent. The committee will examine the demand for an increase in remunerative returns to sugarcane farmers taking into account cultivation costs and other relevant factors. It will also examine the demand for revision of the X-mil minimum selling price of sugar in the context of sugar production costs, market realizations and the financial viability of sugar mills. Other key areas of examination will include existing mechanisms for testing and reporting sugar recovery and prevailing sugarcane weightment practices at sugar mills. The committee will also examine prevailing practices relating to deductions from the weight of mechanically harvested sugarcane and assess the extent of actual losses, if any, arising from mechanized harvesting. It will evaluate the financial impact of the present practices on the cane price payable to farmers and may consider any other related matters deemed necessary.
