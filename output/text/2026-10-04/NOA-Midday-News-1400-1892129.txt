PM Setu aims to enhance the overall quality and relevance of vocational training by transforming government India's industrial training institute's ITIs into modern industry-aligned training institutions. The scheme has a total outlay of over 60,000 crore rupees comprising 30,000 crore rupees from the center, 20,000 crore rupees from states and 10,000 crore rupees from industry. ITIs provide vocational training to equip youth with practical skills for employment and industry. Under the Craftsman Training Scheme, CTS training is offered through government and private industrial training institutes in engineering and non-engineering trades.
