The curtain has come down on the Asian Games with a dazzling closing ceremony at Aichi Nagoya in Japan bringing more than two weeks of action-packed competition across 43 disciplines to an end. The ceremony featured the traditional parade of athletes, the lowering of the Asian Games flag and the formal handover of ceremonial torch and flag to Doha, the host city for the 2030 edition. Proudly carrying the Indian tri-colour for the closing ceremony was India's women's flag bearer and shooting star Neeraudhanda. India signed off from the Games in fourth place in the overall medal standings behind China, Japan and South Korea.
