Various trade associations across New Delhi have urged Prime Minister Narendra Modi to intervene to protect the livelihoods following heavy financial losses caused by recent protests. In a joint letter written to the Prime Minister, the trade bodies highlighted that the protests calls surrounding the 10th of this month created an atmosphere of disruption and panic scaring customers away from major markets for four to five days both before for, and after the event expressing concern over the ongoing festive season, the business bodies urged the Prime Minister, the central government and the judiciary to immediately ban all protests and marches in Delhi's commercial hubs to safeguard the public and local economy. The trade bodies demanded that financial damages be assessed and recovered directly from protest organizers. The Ministry of Home Affairs has introduced the Census Amendment Rules 2026 under the Census Act 1948. The ministry issued a Gazette notification in this regard today. The amendment revises the list of eligible enumerators to include teachers, government officials, local authority staff and other persons considered suitable by the respective state governments or union territory administrations. Under the revised rules, teachers from government, local authority, aided and unaided private educational institutions as well as institutions run by societies or trusts can be appointed as enumerators. The amendment also includes clerks and other officials working in the central and state governments, local authorities, and various establishments.
