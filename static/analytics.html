Analytics dashboard

Scale, coverage, ingestion health and retrieval performance of VaniStream, computed from the live database on every request. Metrics that are not recorded yet say so instead of showing a number.

Dataset

Indexed documents in the selected period and filters (publication date for articles, broadcast date for bulletins).

Coverage

Dates in the configured reporting window that have at least one indexed record (content filters apply).

Content

What is indexed, when it was published or broadcast, and how it is distributed.

Indexed documents over time

Articles and broadcasts per period, by publication / broadcast date.

Broadcasts by type

Indexed bulletins per programme (from the NewsOnAIR title).

Genres

Primary genre from the taxonomy (a bulletin counts once per genre it covers).

Languages

Indexed documents by language.

NewsOnAIR categories

The publisher's own article categories, preserved as published.

Ingestion

Jobs started in the selected period (job timestamps converted to the reporting timezone).

Job history

Jobs per period by outcome.

Daily ingestion activity

Documents processed, skipped as duplicates and failed, by job start date.

Processing funnel

Summed over the jobs in range. Transcription does not apply to articles.

Retrieval (RAG)

Questions answered by the assistant in the selected period. Recorded per request: timings, filters, retrieved document ids and citation use. The question text is not stored.

Query volume

Questions per period.

Latency

Average retrieval, generation and end-to-end time per period.

Queries by genre

Genre chosen as scope, or detected in the question ("all" = no genre).
